QUICK START - Adding Video Background
======================================

STEP 1: Get a Video
-------------------
Download a free video from:
- Pexels: https://www.pexels.com/videos/
- Pixabay: https://pixabay.com/videos/

Search for: "city", "urban", "infrastructure", or "abstract"

STEP 2: Rename the Video
-------------------------
Rename your downloaded video to: background.mp4

STEP 3: Place the Video
------------------------
Copy background.mp4 to this folder:
salamtak_web/assets/videos/

STEP 4: Test
------------
1. Open the website in your browser
2. Login and go to the dashboard
3. You should see your video playing in the background!

NOTES:
------
- Video will loop automatically
- Video is muted by default
- On mobile devices, video is disabled for performance
- If video fails to load, an animated gradient will show instead
- Recommended video size: under 10MB
- Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
